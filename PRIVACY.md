# Privacy Policy — Meditation Timer

*Last updated: 22 August 2026*

Meditation Timer is a simple meditation clock. It is built to know as little
about you as possible.

## What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no
tracking of any kind. Your settings (chosen duration, gong intervals, the
koan option) and the koan rotation are stored only on your device and never
leave it. The gong sounds are synthesized on your device; the app itself
makes no network requests.

## Services provided by Google

Two features rely on Google software already on your device:

- **Fonts.** The app's typefaces (Cormorant Garamond, Jost) are loaded
  through Google Play services' font provider. This may cause Google Play
  services to download the font files from Google. If the provider is not
  available, the app falls back to system fonts.
- **Tips.** The optional tip jar uses Google Play's billing system. Payment
  is handled entirely by Google Play; the app never sees your payment
  details, name, or address. Google's processing of purchases is covered by
  the [Google Play Terms of Service](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/)
  and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Permissions

- **Notifications** — shows a quiet, ongoing notification while a session
  runs, as Android requires for a foreground service.
- **Keep the device awake** — keeps the clock and gongs precise while the
  screen is off during a session.
